# Privacy

**Effective date:** 2026-07-21

Agent Fieldbook is a skills-only plugin. It does not include an MCP server, connector, hook, analytics client, account system, or background process. The plugin itself does not collect, transmit, sell, or store personal data.

The host product may read or edit files and access tools when the user explicitly asks it to perform a task. Those actions are controlled by the host's permissions, the user's connected services, and the applicable Anthropic or OpenAI privacy terms—not by this repository.

The pack links to official third-party documentation, courses, tutorials, and videos. Visiting those sites is subject to their privacy policies.

Do not add credentials, private transcripts, personal data, or confidential source material to issues or contributions. Report a privacy concern privately using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
